# Blind coder A: methods and disagreement-risk note

I coded estimands directly from the pinned primary-source bundle, treating figures and appendices as source text and never consulting the audit manuscript, repository README, scripts, released grids, tests, or prior notes. One CSV row represents one reported headline coefficient or one materially distinct coefficient-generating panel. I separated pooled correlations, task-specific correlations, means of task correlations, checkpoint panels, perturbation panels, and appendix robustness panels when their displayed-cell structure changed. Counts describe the paired values entering a coefficient, not rollout/episode counts unless the source explicitly correlates episode-level values. I did not infer independent training runs from policy names, checkpoints, seeds, or lineages; unresolved counts are '?'. Per-point success-rate intervals were not coded as uncertainty on r.

All 26 pinned papers are represented. The CSV contains source-fact fields, estimand fields, optional axis-specific sensitivity counts, coder_id=blind-a, coding_date=2026-07-23, and a pipe-delimited judgment_fields list for every field whose value required interpretation.

## Highest disagreement risks

- **Means versus pooled coefficients:** SimFoundry, Cosmos-Surg-dVRK, Colosseum V2, A Practical Recipe, and WorldEval explicitly average task/dimension correlations in some panels. Their underlying displayed cells are recorded, but another coder may count only the number of component correlations rather than the paired cells that generate them.
- **Pooled crossed panels:** Digital Cousins, WorldEval's unseen-background appendix, dWorldEval RoboTwin/real panels, REALM overall, and Mem-World require judgment about whether all described policy-task-condition cells enter the printed coefficient. I preserved the ambiguity in fact_ambiguity.
- **Figure-only or internally inconsistent labels:** MolmoSpaces prose reports pick Pearson/Spearman 0.96/0.98 while Fig. 11 prints R=0.96 and rho=1.00; both are retained. EmbodiedSplat labels its statistic “SRCC” without clearly expanding the coefficient family. WEAVER main text uses rho for a Pearson value rounded to 0.87, while Appendix Table 8 distinguishes Pearson=0.863 and Spearman=0.870.
- **Checkpoint versus policy terminology:** SC3-Eval calls seven entries policies/checkpoints, DreamDojo plots six checkpoints from one policy training trajectory, real2sim-eval plots architecture-spanning checkpoints, and Cosmos-Surg-dVRK uses half/full checkpoints for three families. I coded the observable checkpoint axis and left training_runs unknown.
- **Selection:** RoboWorld includes only open-sourced policies and all-three-view episodes; OSCAR manually retains 65 calibrated sessions; MolmoSpaces relies on external-policy overlap; Gemini/Veo selects its strongest nominal policy for the single-policy OOD-axis panel. These selection rules affect interpretability but do not make the fixed-panel coefficient undefined.
- **Unsupported superpopulation inference:** None of the 72 coded rows defines a target sampling population and supplies axis-appropriate uncertainty sufficient for new-policy, new-task, or crossed superpopulation inference. P-values and bootstrap/CI displays are recorded where present, but the coefficients remain valid finite-panel descriptions.
