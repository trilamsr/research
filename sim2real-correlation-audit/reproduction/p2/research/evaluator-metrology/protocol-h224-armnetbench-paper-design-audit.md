## Fixed evidence units

Code each unit as `supported`, `partial`, `absent_from_fixed_scope`, or
`not_assessed`, with exact source locations and a distinction between what
the authors state and what this audit infers:

1. immutable paper-to-dataset/repository identity;
2. versioned active policy roster and immutable evaluated checkpoints;
3. standalone per-policy physical execution rather than partner/history
   mixtures;
4. task/context definition and allocation timing relative to policy choice;
5. assignment, randomization, or execution-order law;
6. physical reset/restoration, acceptance, carryover, and intervention
   handling;
7. stable episode/session/cluster identity and dependence unit;
8. outcome rubric, horizon, abort/missingness, and evaluator identity;
9. complete public per-trial artifact and outcome linkage;
10. uncertainty method that matches the declared dependence/assignment unit;
11. a pre-execution simulator/world-model evaluator that can score every
    candidate without corresponding physical-execution media; and
12. cost/capacity information relevant to the operational design.

## Fixed H022 and P2 decisions

H022 may upgrade only if the new paper supplies source-native evidence that
closes every existing H028 blocker: immutable checkpoints, standalone binary
rubric/horizon, pre-execution evaluator timing, complete panel/linkage,
dependence identity, and authority-compatible public access. Corresponding
real-video scoring remains post-execution and cannot be relabeled.

For P2:

- code a **positive design contrast** only if the paper documents context
  commitment/allocation before policy assignment plus auditable assignment,
  reset, order, and dependence evidence sufficient for the claimed action;
- code an **adverse/mismatch contrast** if it evaluates multiple policies but
  lacks or reverses one of those load-bearing conditions; and
- code **context only** if the paper supplies useful infrastructure without a
  source-supported P2 identification claim.

Do not add the system to P2 merely because it is new or parallel.

## Outcome boundary

The paper is source-selected from title metadata, not performance direction.
Inspect design/methods, data, limitations, and artifact statements only.
Do not extract, tabulate, compare, or interpret policy performance values,
rankings, success rates, or headline outcomes. If a fixed design passage
incidentally contains a numeric performance value, record the exposure and do
not use it.

The existing H028 source is already unsuitable for H022 because its available
evaluator consumes corresponding real-execution media. H224 therefore cannot
create a confirmatory held-out H022 case from this paper.

- readable PDF/source archive with complete extraction;
- source section inventory;
- rendered-page legibility for every relied-on PDF location; and
- one-to-one coverage of all 12 evidence units.

Before a material P2 or H022 consequence is relied upon, independently
challenge the source locations, least-permissive coding, action/order/reset
semantics, outcome-nonselection boundary, and whether the new evidence changes
the existing H028/H178 dispositions.

## Stop rules

Stop without paper promotion if the source adds only capacity/platform
description, corresponding-video evaluation, or unlinked aggregates. Do not
open linked code/data not already governed by H028 unless the paper identifies
a materially new exact artifact and a separately fixed protocol authorizes
it. Do not contact authors, authenticate, accept terms, run models, open
sealed labels, field robots, or publish.
