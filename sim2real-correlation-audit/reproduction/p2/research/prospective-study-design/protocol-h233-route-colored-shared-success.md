# Protocol: H233 route-colored shared-success identification and regret

## Status and outcome exposure

This protocol was fixed on 2026-07-28 after external review identified that
H231 routes every pair through context A and therefore does not test
candidate-dependent routing. Before this protocol was written, the reviewer
had supplied a three-policy route pattern and the author had verified its
opposite-winner and minimax claims. H233 is therefore review-triggered,
outcome-exposed exploratory work. It may repair scope and supply a
construction-specific theorem, but it is not confirmatory evidence and does
not establish novelty.

## Question

When pairwise half-credit scores are generated by shared binary policy
outcomes, what does a candidate-dependent route pattern identify about a
common-context policy winner, and what ex-ante minimax-regret action follows
when the target remains partially identified?

## Model and target

There are \(K\geq3\) policies and finitely many contexts \(c\), with fixed
target weights \(\alpha_c>0\), \(\sum_c\alpha_c=1\). Let
\(x_i^c=E[Y_i(c)]\in[0,1]\), where \(Y_i(c)\) is binary success. Half-credit
comparison satisfies

\[
E[Z_{ij}(c)]=\tfrac12+\tfrac12(x_i^c-x_j^c).
\]

Context \(c\)'s observed route graph has oriented incidence matrix \(E_c\).
The routed observations identify

\[
E_c x^c=d_c,\qquad 0\leq x^c\leq1.
\]

The common-target mean is \(\mu=\sum_c\alpha_c x^c\). For any fixed
opponent-reference distribution \(r\),

\[
V_i=\tfrac12+\tfrac12(\mu_i-r^\top\mu).
\]

The action is a lottery \(p\) chosen before the compatible completion. Loss is
regret to the completion-specific best policy.

## Fixed claims to test

1. The null space of \(E_c\) has one constant-offset degree of freedom per
   connected component of the route graph.
2. If every positive-target-weight route graph is connected, all pairwise
   differences in \(\mu\), and therefore its winner set, are identified.
3. If a positive-weight route graph is disconnected, component offsets can
   leave target differences unidentified whenever the compatible box has
   sufficient interior slack. Disconnection alone is not claimed sufficient
   at every boundary point.
4. Exact identification is equivalent to zero compatible width for every
   pairwise target difference \(\mu_i-\mu_j\).
5. Opponent-reference weights cancel from regret:

   \[
   \mathcal R(p)=\tfrac12\max_w\sup_{\{x^c\}}
   (e_w-p)^\top\sum_c\alpha_c x^c.
   \]

6. Each inner support function is an LP. With free \(\lambda_{wc}\) and
   \(u_{wc}\geq0\), the joint minimax problem has the dual form

   \[
   \begin{aligned}
   \min\quad &t\\
   \text{s.t.}\quad&
   t\geq\tfrac12\sum_c(d_c^\top\lambda_{wc}+\mathbf1^\top u_{wc})
   &&\forall w,\\
   &E_c^\top\lambda_{wc}+u_{wc}\geq
   \alpha_c(e_w-p)&&\forall w,c,\\
   &p\geq0,\quad\mathbf1^\top p=1,\quad u_{wc}\geq0.
   \end{aligned}
   \]

7. Known-answer candidate-dependent-routing case:
   - \(K=3\), equal target weights on A and B;
   - edges 1--3 and 2--3 route through A with \(d_A=0\);
   - edge 1--2 routes through B with \(d_B=1/2\);
   - compatible completions include \(x^B=(1/2,0,0)\) and
     \(x^B=(1/2,0,1)\), giving opposite unique target winners 1 and 3;
   - the unique ex-ante minimax lottery is \((2/3,0,1/3)\) with regret
     \(1/12\).

## Computation and gates

- Verify incidence-rank/component identities for every undirected graph on
  \(K=3,\ldots,6\), and a deterministic sample for \(K=7,8\).
- Enumerate the known-answer compatible-polytope vertices with exact rational
  arithmetic.
- Solve the induced finite robust LP numerically and require agreement with
  the exact optimizer and value to \(10^{-9}\).
- Use a separate Node implementation that does not import or execute the
  Python producer. It must verify the binary half-tie identity, route
  observational equivalence, opposite winners, and the exact minimax result
  on a rational simplex grid containing the proposed optimizer.
- Mutation controls must fail if the B-route difference, route assignment,
  optimizer, or value is changed.

## Interpretation and stop conditions

Passing H233 supports only this shared-binary-success, finite-context model
and the declared target/loss. It does not show that a named robot benchmark
uses this response model, that its route graph is known, or that the proposed
action is operationally acceptable. Failure of the theorem, LP equivalence,
known answer, or independent reconstruction blocks promotion of a structured
routing claim. A prior-art collision narrows or removes novelty language but
does not change a correct mathematical result.
